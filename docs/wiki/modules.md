## Audio

| Class | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| [`UGameAudioSubsystem`](../../Game_01%205.8/Source/Game_01/Private/Audio/GameAudioSubsystem.cpp) | A world subsystem that exists only in game worlds (`ShouldCreateSubsystem` rejects editor preview worlds). `Play(EGameSound)` / `PlayIn(WorldContext, Sound)` plays one of the 11 sound effects as 2D sound at master x effects volume; `StartMusic(EMusicTrack)` starts the camp or run loop (never restarts the one already playing, fades the other out over 0.5 s). The assets are loaded in `Initialize` from `Sounds`, `CampMusic` and `RunMusic` in `Director Tuning`. A missing asset is a silent no-op: counted per sound and logged once (`AUDIO first request ...`, `AUDIOCFG ...` at start). `Attack`, `EnemyHit` and `EnemyDeath` are crowd sounds: at most `MaxCrowdSoundsPerSecond` (16) start per second of real time, the rest are counted as capped. `GetBusVolume` / `SetBusVolume` hold the three volume levels (master, music, effects) in a file-static array that outlives the world; negative means "use the `Director Tuning` value". `SetBusVolume` also re-scales the music that is playing. Callers: weapon component, enemy, progression, run outcome, camp meta, extraction zone, loot container, and the two game modes (music); the Esc menu changes the volumes |
| `EGameSound`, `EMusicTrack`, `EAudioBus` | The 11 effects (Attack, EnemyHit, EnemyDeath, Pickup, LevelUp, Cut, StarUp, Extraction, PlayerDeath, Upgrade, ExtractStart), the two tracks (Camp, Run) and the three buses (Master, Music, Effects) |

Not here: choosing which sound plays when (every call site decides), and the volume controls themselves ([`UPauseMenuWidget`](#ui)).

## Loot: Loot, Bag and Heat

| Class | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| [`ULootBagComponent`](../../Game_01%205.8/Source/Game_01/Private/Loot/LootBagComponent.cpp) | Bag: wood and stone are counted separately and each takes `ceil(amount / IronPerSlot)` slots, each lost film reel takes 1 slot, total capacity `BagCapacitySlots` (default 6, `IronPerSlot` default 10). `TryAddLoot` is atomic: when it would not fit, nothing is added and a "Pickup refused: Bag is full" toast is posted. Picking up a reel raises heat. A reel cannot be used: it only does something when it is carried out alive (see `URunOutcomeSubsystem`). `BeginPlay` pushes the tuning into `ULootHeatSubsystem` and logs a `LOOTCFG` line saying whether the numbers came from the tables or the C++ defaults, because a missing table falls back silently. Wood is still stored in `IronCount` (`GetWoodCount()` is the readable name) |
| [`ALootContainer`](../../Game_01%205.8/Source/Game_01/Private/Loot/LootContainer.cpp) | Searchable container, each searchable only once; searching completes instantly and plays the `Pickup` sound. A supply crate yields wood and is drawn as a tree stump, a vault yields stone and is drawn as a boulder (both from the `RPGTinyFantasyForest` pack; without it a cube). The roll is made on the first try and remembered: when the bag is full nothing is consumed and a retry does not re-roll (so a full bag cannot be used to re-roll the loot). If another container filled the same reel type into the bag meanwhile, the remembered reel is dropped and only the wood or stone is given. A reel is only rolled when `ULootConfig::PickRandomRelic` finds one that can still drop. A searched container is flattened to a quarter of its height. Default rows (field names say Iron, the amount is wood or stone): Supply Crate 3-6 with a 20% reel chance, Vault 10-20 with a 100% reel chance |
| [`ULootHeatSubsystem`](../../Game_01%205.8/Source/Game_01/Private/Loot/LootHeatSubsystem.cpp) | Global heat from 0 to `MaxHeat` (default 100, +15 per reel). `GetSpawnPressureMultiplier()` interpolates linearly between `SpawnPressureAtZeroHeat` (1.0) and `SpawnPressureAtMaxHeat` (3.0) by normalized heat, and spawning reads it |
| [`ULootConfig`](../../Game_01%205.8/Source/Game_01/Private/Loot/LootConfig.cpp) | Loot config: reel table, container table, tuning table. `CreateFromSettings` builds one from the three loot tables in `Director Tuning` (`LootTuningTable`, `LootContainerTable`, `ReelTable`). The `ULootConfig` data asset itself is optional (`ULootBagComponent::Config`). A missing or empty table falls back to the inline defaults in the constructor. In those defaults Fast Cut names `Haste`, Wide Shot `Magnet` and Chain Scene `Chain` (the shipped `DT_Reels` maps the same, per the earlier check; the asset is binary and was not re-read); a reel whose `UnlocksOption` is `None` never drops. `PickRandomRelic` rolls only reels that can still drop, weighted by `DropWeight` (all weights zero = uniform) |
| [`LootTypes.h`](../../Game_01%205.8/Source/Game_01/Public/Loot/LootTypes.h) | Enums and data rows: `ERelicType` (display names Fast Cut, Wide Shot, Chain Scene; the C++ names `Swiftstep`, `Warbrand`, `Aegis` predate the rename and are kept for save compatibility), `ELootContainerType`, `ELootResource` (Wood, Stone), `EBagAddResult`, `FRelicRow` (`UnlocksOption`, `DropWeight`, `IsDroppable`), `FLootContainerRow`, `FLootTuning`, plus the pure helpers `FReelUnlocks` (`AddOption`, `OptionsForReels`), which touch no UObjects so tests need no world |

| [`AGunBullet`, `AOrbitOrb`](../../Game_01%205.8/Source/Game_01/Private/Combat/GunBullet.cpp) | `AGunBullet` flies straight, moves in steps of at most 40 cm, damages the first enemy within `HitRadius` (through `FindNearestEnemy`) and disappears there or at the end of its range; it waits while a level-up offer is open. `AOrbitOrb` is a glowing ball with no logic: the weapon component moves it, and `UBossRejectsSubsystem` reuses it for the boss's balls |

## Progression: In-Run Growth

Brief v3: the level-up is "take one of three, optional cut" (v2 was "cut one, keep two"). Three options are offered, the player takes one, which adds one copy of a weapon or passive, and may first cut one, once per level-up. Copies are also the item's level.

| Class | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| [`URunProgressionSubsystem`](../../Game_01%205.8/Source/Game_01/Private/Progression/RunProgressionSubsystem.cpp) | XP and level (requirement to leave level `L` = `BaseXp x Growth^(L-1)`, defaults 30 and 1.3). On a level-up `GenerateOffer` builds a pool of everything that is available (an option that starts locked is available only once a lost film reel has unlocked the id equal to its table row name: `Chain`, `Haste`, `Magnet`), not cut for good and not at the copy cap, shuffles it, moves options unlocked since the player last saw an offer to the front (marked `bIsNewUnlock`, shown as `NEW!`; the list comes from `UCampStockSubsystem::TakeNewOptions`) and offers 3. `ChooseOption(Index)` applies the taken one and then checks for another level-up; `CutFromOffer(Index)` removes one entry from the open offer and from the pool for the rest of the run, once per level-up, while `PermanentCutsLeft` > 0 (`PermanentCutsForPool(pool) = max(pool - MinPoolSize, 0)`: the 13 options of a new save and `MinPoolSize` 4 give 9 permanent cuts, and each unlocked option adds one option and one cut, up to 16 and 12); after that `CanCutFromOffer` is false. With fewer than 3 options in the pool there is nothing to cut, the player simply gets what is left. Every cut goes to `GetCuttingRoomFloor()`, which `UBossRejectsSubsystem` reads. Also: weapon levels and stars, passive stacks, the results the other systems read (`GetDamageMultiplier`, `GetMoveSpeedMultiplier`, `GetXpGainMultiplier`, `GetAttackSpeedMultiplier`, `GetMagnetRadius`, `GetRangeMultiplier`, `GetDamageReduction` capped at 75%, `GetRegenPerSecond`, `GetCritChance` capped at 90%), `GetLoadoutSummary()`, `GetOfferCards()` and `GetOfferDescriptions()` for the HUD, pickup magnetism and collection. An enemy dies without dropping XP while an offer is pending, and a Boss drops 10x XP. Reset every run by `ResetProgression()`, and the run starts with a level 1 Blade. Plays `LevelUp`, `Cut`, `StarUp` and (gem collected) `Pickup`. `DebugGrantWeapon` and `DebugCut` are for the probes |
| [`AXpPickup`](../../Game_01%205.8/Source/Game_01/Private/Progression/XpPickup.cpp) | XP gem (small green cube), does not tick or collide, registered with the subsystem, which attracts (radius `GetMagnetRadius()`: `BaseMagnetRadius` 600 from the XP curve row plus the Magnet passive; speed 900) and collects it (radius 80) every frame, scaled by the Greed multiplier |
| [`ProgressionTypes.h`](../../Game_01%205.8/Source/Game_01/Public/Progression/ProgressionTypes.h) | `EWeaponType` (Blade, Bolt, Nova, Chain, Gun, Orbit), `EPassiveType` (Swiftness, Might, Vitality, Greed, Haste, Magnet, Reach, Armor, Regen, Crit), `FXpCurveRow` (including `BaseMagnetRadius`), `FWeaponRow` (`ChainJumps`, `ChainRange`, `ProjectileSpeed`, `ProjectileCount`, `bStartsLocked`), `FPassiveRow` (one effect field per passive, `bStartsLocked`), `FOptionUnlock::IsAvailable` (the pure unlock rule) and `FStarTuning` (pure functions, unit tested): 3 copies make 2 stars and 9 make 3 stars (also the most copies one item can hold); weapon damage x2 / x4 and reach x1.25 / x1.6; passive total effect x1.5 / x2.5; `MinPoolSize` 4. A passive's total = `stacks x per-stack amount x star multiplier`, and Vitality heals by however much its bonus grew, including a star-up jump |

The four passives added late (Reach, Armor, Regen, Crit) and the two weapons (Gun, Orbit) have their rows only as C++ defaults in `RunProgressionSubsystem.cpp` unless `DT_Weapons` / `DT_Passives` define them (the assets are binary and were not read here).

## Editing: Pre-Run Editing

[`UFilmCardSubsystem`](../../Game_01%205.8/Source/Game_01/Private/Editing/FilmCardSubsystem.cpp) (a world subsystem, rebuilt for every level): film cards in two slots (`MaxSlots`). The card rows come from `FilmCardTable` (`FFilmCardRow` and `EFilmCardEffect` are in [`FilmCardTypes.h`](../../Game_01%205.8/Source/Game_01/Public/Editing/FilmCardTypes.h)); with no table there are no cards. Card effects: `ForceHorde` (spawn a batch of Grunts at the start of the run, default 40, ignoring the alive cap), `ForceBoss` (spawn a Boss at the start of the run, which also switches on the cut-fed boss attacks), and payout multipliers multiply together (default 1.5 each). The selection is stored in `UCampMetaSubsystem::PendingFilmCards`, so it survives `OpenLevel`, and is restored in `Initialize`. It is cleared when a run ends, so a choice never leaks into the next run.

## Flow: Save Game

## Player: Hero

[`AHeroCharacter`](../../Game_01%205.8/Source/Game_01/Private/Player/HeroCharacter.cpp): WASD to move (walk speed 600 times the Swiftness multiplier), Space to dodge (0.18 s dash at 1800 cm/s with 0.25 s invulnerability, cooldown 0.8 s), E to search the nearest unsearched container within 250 cm, 1 through 3 to **take** a level-up option and 4 through 6 to **cut** it while the level-up panel is shown, Esc to open or close the Esc menu (ignored while an offer is showing), K for debug suicide. Movement, dodge and search are all blocked while no run is active, the hero is dead or an offer is pending (`CanAct`). `ReceiveEnemyDamage` applies the Armor reduction before health; `Tick` clamps the hero to the floor and applies Regen. The body is the `RPGHeroSquad` model with sword, shield and a run or frozen-walk animation (a grey cylinder without the pack). `BeginPlay` calls `StartRun()` for run-start initialization (clear enemies and the kill count, reset the spawner, heat, bag and progression, set max health to base plus the Clinic bonus, start a new run, apply the slotted film cards), then `CreateHud()` (the HUD and the Esc menu in run context, with the cursor shown). Lost film reels cannot be used.

| [`UPlayAreaSubsystem`](../../Game_01%205.8/Source/Game_01/Private/Game/PlayAreaSubsystem.cpp) | At world begin play finds the walkable floor (the union of every static mesh actor that is wide in both directions, half-size at least 1000 cm, and thin, height at most 20% of its half-width) and offers `ClampToArea(Location, Margin)`. It also triggers the forest look: when the game mode is the run or camp one it calls `AForestDressing::Build` |
| [`AForestDressing`](../../Game_01%205.8/Source/Game_01/Private/Game/ForestDressing.cpp) | The look of the floor: a large grass land mass under the play area, a ring of 160 trees, 40 rocks and 10 cliffs outside it, and 500 grass tufts and flowers inside it (instanced meshes, no collision, fixed random seed). It hides the old floor actor without removing it (the old floor keeps the collision). Returns false and spawns nothing if the `RPGTinyFantasyForest` land mesh is missing |

## UI

See [ui.md](ui.md). Classes: [`URunHudWidget`](../../Game_01%205.8/Source/Game_01/Private/UI/RunHudWidget.cpp) (HP, level and XP, loadout, heat, bag, extraction bar, the three-card level-up panel with Take and Cut buttons, result screen), [`UCampWidget`](../../Game_01%205.8/Source/Game_01/Private/UI/CampWidget.cpp) (camp panel), [`UPauseMenuWidget`](../../Game_01%205.8/Source/Game_01/Private/UI/PauseMenuWidget.cpp) (the Esc menu, below), [`UHudMessageSubsystem`](../../Game_01%205.8/Source/Game_01/Private/UI/HudMessageSubsystem.cpp) (toasts) and the shared building blocks in [`DirectorUi.h`](../../Game_01%205.8/Source/Game_01/Public/UI/DirectorUi.h).

**The Esc menu** (`UPauseMenuWidget`, one widget for both screens): Resume, Settings (three volume rows in 10% steps, kept for this session only), Quit game, a controls reminder and the credits (the music and the WW2 buildings are CC BY). In a run it also has "Return to camp (lose bag)" and it really pauses the game while open. The return button needs a second click within 3 seconds (real time) to confirm, then ends the run as a death (the bag is lost, nothing is unlocked), posts a toast and opens `L_Camp`; if the run already ended it goes back at once. In the camp it only offers Resume, Settings and Quit and does not pause.
