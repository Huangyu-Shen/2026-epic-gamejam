# Smoke check: story 010 number retune (first pass), 2026-11-14

What this is: a first pass on the numbers, measured with a bot and the packaged build. It is **not** the timed human playthrough that story 010's criteria call for; that part is still open and is listed at the end.

## 1. The problem, measured

A bot (`-PaceProbe`) plays whole runs: the hero walks in a circle with the weapons firing, always cuts the first level-up option, and the run is sped up (game seconds below). It is a rough stand-in for a player who keeps moving and never dodges.

| Numbers | Runs | Result (game seconds) |
|---|---|---|
| **Before:** spawn base 0.8/s, ramp +1.5/s per minute, grunt contact damage 10, hero health 100 | 3 | bot died at 33, 91 and 97 s |

At 97 s the spawn rate was already 3.1 grunts per second, and nothing in the game heals the hero, so a 3 to 10 minute run was not possible.

## 2. The sweep (editor build, probe-only overrides, 2 runs each)

| Set | Spawn base | Ramp / min | Contact damage | Hero health | Bot result |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A (before) | 0.8 | 1.5 | 10 | 100 | died 91 s, 33 s |
| B | 0.6 | 0.8 | 8 | 150 | died 181 s, survived 600 s |
| **C (chosen)** | **0.5** | **0.6** | **6** | **150** | died 283 s, survived 600 s |
| D | 0.5 | 0.4 | 6 | 200 | survived 600 s, 600 s |
| E | 0.6 | 0.5 | 7 | 150 | survived 600 s, 600 s |

D and E let the bot live for the full 10 minutes in every run, which would leave a human with no threat. C sits between "dies in two minutes" and "never dies".

## 3. What was written to the data tables (approved by the user)

| Table, row | Value | From | To |
|---|---|---|---|
| `DT_SpawnTuning`, Spawn | BaseRate | 0.8 | 0.5 |
| `DT_SpawnTuning`, Spawn | RampPerMinute | 1.5 | 0.6 |
| `DT_EnemyTuning`, Grunt | ContactDamage | 10 | 6 |
| `DT_HealthTuning`, Hero | MaxHealth | 100 | 150 |

Nothing else was changed. XP curve, weapon numbers, boss, building costs and film cards are as they were.

## 4. After the change

- **Editor, 3 bot runs on the new tables:** died at 566 s (level 24); survived 600 s (level 17, health 72 of 600, 65 enemies alive); and one run (run 2) that **never finished**: its log ends at 360 s with level 9, 200 enemies alive (the cap) and the hero at 24 of 150 health, unchanged for two game minutes, when my 5-minute real-time limit stopped it. I do not know whether the bot got stuck or the game ran very slowly with 200 enemies, and a hero at constant health among 200 enemies is odd and worth a look. The log is `editor-pace.txt` in `production/qa/evidence/story-010-number-retune/`.
- **Packaged Development build, fresh save, 1 bot run:** survived 600 s. Health fell from 600 to 240 over the last five minutes with 42 grunts alive and a spawn rate of 6.4 per second, so enemies still threaten at the 10 minute mark. A first star-up happened by level 5 (Greed at 3 copies).
- **Editor and package read the same numbers.** The `TUNING` lines match exactly: hero 150, grunt contact 6, spawn 0.50 and +0.60. The old mismatch (hero health 100 in the package, 150 in the editor) came from the editor's save having Clinic upgrades. On a fresh save both now read 150.
- **Frame rate, packaged build, 1920x1080 windowed, 151 enemies:** average 118 FPS, worst single frame 13 FPS. The worst frame is a one-off hitch when the probe spawns the whole horde at once; the earlier (editor) measurement on Day 1 had a worst frame of 50 FPS. Advisory: the average is far above 60, the worst frame is not.

## 5. Not done: needs a person (story 010 criteria)

- Two runs inside 15 minutes, each between 3 and 10 minutes, from a deleted save.
- A first-time player can extract on the starting cards (the bot never extracts).
- Two building upgrades payable from two extracted runs (costs and Iron income were not measured).
- The Cut Knight card visibly changes the run, and the boss (1,500 health, 35 contact damage) can be beaten by a normal build.
- A reel in the bag makes the trip visibly harder through heat, and a careful player can still extract.
