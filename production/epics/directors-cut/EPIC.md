# Epic: Director's Cut (MVP)

> **Source**: `design/game-brief.md` (scope gate: `design/prd/master-prd.md`)
> **Status**: Active

**Goal**: A fantasy top-down horde-survival game. Before a run the player edits the difficulty with film cards; the more treasure is found in the run, the stronger the enemies get; the player decides whether to use treasure to survive or carry it out to build the camp.

**Scope**: the brief's 7 MVP features, one story each, in the brief's Build order. Anything outside the MVP list stays out (see the brief's Out of scope).
